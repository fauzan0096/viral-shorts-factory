# Channel plan — 4 best picks

Ye 4 formats aaj ke Shorts/Reels landscape me sabse zyada repeatable (roz banane layak) hain.
Sabse pehle **#1** start karo. Har channel ke liye pipeline profile ready hai.

---

## 1. Roz Ek Junoon — motivation / discipline
**Profile:** `channels/motivation.json`

- **Format:** 35-40s, ek strong hook, 5-7 short lines, last me CTA. Hindi male voice + cinematic b-roll + burned-in captions.
- **Kyun viral:** Retain karta hai (people finish motivational shorts), evergreen topic bank unlimited,
  loop pe sunne wale audience comments karte hain ("ye sach hai").
- **Aaj ka trend kaise catch hota hai:** YouTube pe "discipline motivation hindi", "study motivation hindi" me
  7 din ke high-velocity videos ke keywords nikalte hain; jo word trend kar raha ho usi pe idea banta hai.
- **B-roll:** gym, rain walk, sunrise silhouette, city night timelapse, study desk.
- **Upload:** roz 08:00 IST. Title pattern: `Discipline ka simple formula | #shorts`.

## 2. Rok Facts — facts / did-you-know
**Profile:** `channels/facts.json`

- **Format:** 30-35s, ek fact = ek video, "99% log ye nahi jaante" hook, 4-5 supporting lines.
- **Kyun viral:** Highest thumbnail CTR, comments me debate ("galat hai bhai") = engagement,
  aur script banana sabse fast (ek fact, 5 line).
- **Rule:** only verified facts. Galat fact = trust gone. Doubt ho to fact hata do.
- **B-roll:** space, ocean, microscope, animals, abstract particles.

## 3. Theek Hai Bhai — relateable comedy
**Profile:** `channels/comedy.json`

- **Format:** 25-30s, POV / expectation-vs-reality one-liners, fast voice, big captions.
- **Kyun viral:** Share-first format (log "ye main hoon" bolke bhejte hain). Cost sabse kam.
- **Warning:** Comedy me copyright sabse zyada lagti hai — **meme audio/movie clips use mat karo**.
  Sirf apna TTS voice + stock b-roll. Joke apna likho, trend se sirf *format* lo.
- **B-roll:** friends laughing, office desk tired, traffic, chai closeup, phone scrolling night.

## 4. Chhoti Kahani — moral story
**Profile:** `channels/story.json`

- **Format:** 45-55s, ek chhoti kahani + last 5 second me sabak. Female voice (Swara) — storytelling ke liye soft.
- **Kyun viral:** Sabse zyada watch-time per view, log save/share karte hain, family audience.
- **Reality:** Ye channel sabse zyada mehnat maangta hai (story quality). Isliye isko #3-4 me rakho, jab
  pehle channel ka rhythm ban jaye.

---

## Pehle 30 din ka plan (channel #1 ke liye)

| Din | Kaam |
|-----|------|
| 1-3 | Setup: `.env`, keys, `--dry-run` se 3 scripts dekho, apne 10 b-roll clips + 1 music track daalo |
| 4-7 | Roz render karo, **private** upload, khud dekho — hook, caption size, voice rate tune karo |
| 8-14 | Public karo, roz 1 video (08:00 IST). Pehle 5-8 videos me algorithm ko audience samajhna hota hai |
| 15-21 | Jo video best chali usi format ko repeat karo (same topic family, naya angle) |
| 22-30 | Naya channel (#2 facts) open karo ya motivation pe hi double down karo |

**Decision rule:** 20-25 videos ke baad Shorts tab dekho — average view duration aur "viewed vs swiped away" %.
Agar 70%+ log dekh rahe hain to format chal raha hai, sirf thumbnails/hooks sharpen karo.

---

## Ek channel ko 3 baar na kholo

1 channel = 1 voice + 1 format + 1 upload time. Roz topic badal sakta hai, format nahi.
Isi liye har profile alag `.json` hai — ek hi pipeline, alag identity.
